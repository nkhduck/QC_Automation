About Us

The Story

DOM (Dreams of Mural) was born from an urgent need within the art-loving community. As people’s lives improved, their needs evolved, moving beyond basic necessities to include a desire for self-expression, community, and creative exploration. Recognizing this, DOM's founders, fueled by their passion for creativity, envisioned a place where anyone could freely express themselves through mural art. They discovered that, while there was interest in a space for community art, there was no place in Da Nang that met these needs. DOM is a welcoming creative space where people can paint on walls without environmental concerns, equipped with all necessary tools. It’s a place for professional artists, beginners, and anyone who loves art to connect, learn, and create lasting memories together. DOM aims to bring out the hidden artist in everyone, creating a vibrant community united by the love of mural painting.
